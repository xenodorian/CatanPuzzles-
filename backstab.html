Rules

  • Every piece faces one of 8 directions (the pointer). An action is a move or a turn.
  • Fighters and Barbarians step 1 square forward and capture whatever enemy is there.
  • Rogues slide any distance forward, but capture only from behind: moving the same way the target faces.
  • Rangers step 1 square any way, never melee, and shoot an enemy 2+ squares away along a clear straight or diagonal line.
  • Barbarians grapple enemies they stand directly behind or beside: a grappled piece can't act.
  • You move the hostage in the enemy's back row (your colour). Win by walking it home to your back row, or by capturing it with one of your own pieces (a rescue).

    How to play

    Backstab is a small tactics game on a 7 by 7 board. Each player has two rows: seven Fighters in front and, behind them, Rogue, Ranger, Barbarian, Hostage, Barbarian, Ranger, Rogue. The three middle rows start empty. Gold moves first.

    • Facing. Every piece except the Hostage faces one of 8 directions (straight or diagonal), shown by its pointer. Everything starts facing the enemy.
    • Your turn. Do one action with one piece: move it, or turn it to face any other direction (turning uses the whole turn). Moving never changes facing.
    • Fighters move one square in the direction they face. If an enemy is on that square, it is captured, whichever way the enemy faces.
    • Rogues slide any number of empty squares in the direction they face, like a queen limited to one line. A Rogue can capture only by a backstab: it may end its slide on an enemy only when it is moving the same way that enemy faces, so it arrives from directly behind. Any other piece in the way just blocks it.
    • Rangers move one square in any of the 8 directions onto an empty square, whatever way they face (facing stays the same). They never capture by moving. Instead, a Ranger may shoot: it removes an enemy piece on one of its 8 straight or diagonal lines that is at least 2 squares away, with every square in between empty. The Ranger stays where it is. A piece right next to a Ranger can't be shot. Rangers still have a facing, so they can be backstabbed and grappled, and a grappled Ranger can't shoot. Turning a Ranger also uses the turn.
    • Barbarians move and capture like Fighters. They also grapple: an enemy piece with a Barbarian directly behind it (the square opposite its facing) or directly beside it (the two squares at right angles to its facing) can't move, turn or capture. A Barbarian in front of a piece or on any diagonal of its facing does not grapple it. For a piece facing NE, behind is SW and the sides are NW and SE. Grappled pieces show a chain and hatching. (Hostages are different: any captor Barbarian on one of the 8 squares around a hostage holds it.) A grappled piece can still be captured.
    • Hostages. The middle of each back row holds the enemy's hostage. It is drawn in its controller's colour, ringed in its captors' colour. You move the hostage sitting in your opponent's back row: one square in any of 8 directions, onto an empty square only. It never captures, and moving it is your whole turn.
    • Held hostages. A hostage can't move while any enemy (captor) Barbarian is next to it. At the start both captor Barbarians stand beside it, so both must leave or be captured first.
    • Winning. You win when your hostage reaches your own back row (the far side from where it started), or when one of your pieces captures your hostage, which rescues it. A Rogue may rescue from any direction, since a hostage has no facing, but a Ranger can't rescue by shooting: hostages can't be shot by anyone and block lines of sight like any piece. Captors can't capture the hostage; it just blocks them.
    • Other endings. A player with no legal action loses. The game is a draw if the same position occurs three times, or after 200 actions without a winner.
    • Tap one of your pieces: green dots are moves, orange rings are captures, red crosshairs are Ranger shots, and anything bright green wins the game. The small arrows around the piece turn it.
    • Notation: files a to g from the left, ranks 1 to 7 from Gold's side. F Fighter, R Rogue, A Ranger, B Barbarian, H Hostage. "x" is a capture, "*" a Ranger shot (the Ranger stays put) and ">NE" a turn.

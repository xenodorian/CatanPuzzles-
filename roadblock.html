Rules

  • Gold links the upper-left port to the lower-right port. Scarlet links upper-right to lower-left.
  • Gold opens with 1 road. After that every turn is 2 roads.
  • Each road must touch one of your ports or roads.
  • A crossroads your road touches is yours: your opponent can never build there.
  • Win by linking your ports, or by cutting your opponent off so no route is left.

    How to play

    Roadblock is the road-building part of a Catan-style game and nothing else: no resources, no dice, just a race to link two harbours while walling off your rival. The terrain is only scenery.

    • Board. An island of 37 hexes. Roads go on the edges between hexes (and along the coast). The corners where edges meet are crossroads.
    • Goal. Gold owns the two gold harbours, upper left and lower right. Scarlet owns the two scarlet harbours, upper right and lower left. Link your two harbours with one unbroken road.
    • Turns. Gold opens with a single road. From then on every turn, for both players, is two roads, like playing a Road Building card each turn. (One road a turn gives the first player a big edge; this order makes it close to even.)
    • Building. Each new road must go on an empty edge and touch your network: one of your harbour crossroads, or the end of one of your roads. Your network may branch.
    • Roadblock rule. A crossroads touched by your road, or at your harbour, belongs to you. Your opponent can never build a road that touches it, the same way a rival settlement stops a road in Catan. Because the two routes have to cross somewhere, only one player can get through each crossing.
    • Winning. You win the moment your harbours are linked, or the moment your opponent has no possible route left between their harbours (cut off). The game checks this after every road and highlights the winning road, or the winner's open route if it ended by a cut-off.
    • No legal road. A player who cannot build loses. This can only happen to a player who is already cut off.
    • Shortest routes in the sidebar show how many more roads each player needs at best.
    • Controls. With a mouse, hover to preview a road and click to build. On a touch screen, tap an edge to preview it and tap it again to build. Dashed lines mark every legal road.

    An original connection game in the spirit of Hex and Shannon's switching game, drawn in a Catan-style island.

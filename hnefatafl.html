Rules

  • Attackers (dark, 24) move first. Defenders (light, 12) protect the King.
  • Every piece moves like a rook. Only the King may stop on the throne or the corners.
  • Capture by sandwiching an enemy between two of yours, or against a corner or hostile throne.
  • King wins by reaching a corner. Attackers win by surrounding the King on 4 sides (3 + throne).

    How to play

    Hnefatafl ("King's table") is the Viking board game. This page plays the Copenhagen rules on an 11 by 11 board.

    • Sides. 24 Attackers (dark) start in T shapes on the four edges. 12 Defenders (light) and the King (gold) start in the middle. Attackers move first.
    • Moving. Every piece, the King included, moves any number of empty squares in a straight line, like a rook. No jumping.
    • Restricted squares. The throne (centre) and the four corners. Only the King may stop on them. Other pieces may pass over the empty throne.
    • Capture. Move a piece so an enemy is sandwiched between it and another of your pieces on the opposite side (in a row or column, not diagonally). The corners are hostile to everyone. The throne is always hostile to Attackers, and hostile to Defenders when it is empty. A piece may safely move between two enemies. The King can help capture.
    • Shieldwall. A row of two or more pieces along the board edge is captured all at once if every piece in it has an enemy directly in front of it, and you close both ends of the row (a corner can close one end). The King is never taken this way.
    • Capturing the King. Attackers must surround the King on all four sides, or on three sides when the fourth is the throne. The King cannot be captured on the board edge.
    • Defenders win when the King reaches any corner.
    • Attackers win by capturing the King, or by surrounding the King and every remaining Defender so none of them can reach the board edge.
    • No moves. A player who cannot move loses.
    • Repetition. Per Copenhagen, perpetual repetition is a loss for the Defenders: if the same position (same side to move) occurs a third time, the Attackers win.
    • Not included: the optional edge-fort win for the Defenders.
    • Tap a piece, then a green dot. An orange dot means the move captures.
    • Notation: files a to k from the left, ranks 1 to 11 from the bottom. K marks the King, x marks captured squares.
